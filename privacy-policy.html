2. Information Collection and Use

We designed Workon – Daily Work Tracker to prioritize your privacy. We do not collect or store your personal information on external servers owned by Bharat Code.

The Application accesses and processes the following types of data:

  • User-entered data: Tasks, titles, tags, streaks, hours worked, and similar information you add manually.
  • Automatically collected information: Device type, operating system version, usage duration, and crash logs (if enabled by your OS).
  • Cloud Backup Data (Optional): If you explicitly enable "Cloud Backup" in settings, the app creates an encrypted copy of your local data.

We do not collect location data, sensitive personal data, or biometric identifiers.

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage
By default, all data created within the Application is stored locally on your device in a secure database. This data is not transmitted to us.

Google Drive™ Backup (Optional)
To prevent data loss, the Application offers an optional feature to back up your data to your personal Google Drive account.

  • Encryption: Before uploading, your data is encrypted using AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) with a password defined by you.
  • Storage Location: Encrypted backups are stored in the hidden "App Data" folder of your Google Drive.
  • Access Control:
    • You: Can restore this data using the Application and your password.
    • Us (Bharat Code): We have no access to your Google Drive, your backup files, or your encryption password. We cannot decrypt your data.
    • Public: These files are hidden from the main Google Drive view and cannot be accessed by other apps.

We follow industry-standard practices to protect data integrity. However, since you hold the encryption password, you are responsible for remembering it. If you lose your password, the backup cannot be restored.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Data
Your locally stored data remains on your device until:

  • You delete specific entries manually within the app.
  • You uninstall the Application (which removes all local data).

Cloud Data
If you use the Cloud Backup feature, the encrypted backup file remains in your Google Drive until:

  • You overwrite it with a new backup.
  • You manually revoke the Application's access to your Drive via your Google Account permissions page.
  • You delete the app data from your Google Drive storage settings.
